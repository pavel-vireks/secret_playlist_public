# Secret Playlist

[Русская версия](README_ru.md) · [Українська версія](README_ua.md)

**For supertesters.** Test vehicles credited for the supertest carry the `secret` tag and get lost
among hundreds of tanks in the carousel. The mod keeps a carousel playlist **"Secret"** with all of them.

**Client:** 2.4.1.0 · **Mod version:** 1.0.0

## Download

- [`secret_playlist.zip`](https://github.com/pavel-vireks/secret_playlist_public/releases/latest/download/secret_playlist.zip) — the mod (`secret_playlist.wotmod`), already laid out in the `mods\2.4.1.0\` folder.

## Installation

Unpack the archive into the game folder (the one with `WorldOfTanks.exe`) and restart the client.

## How it works

- The playlist appears in the regular playlist dropdown above the carousel.
- It contains every vehicle in your garage tagged `secret` that the Random Battles carousel can show.
  The T-44-122 (the supertest reward vehicle) is not included.
- The list updates by itself when new test vehicles are credited or old ones are taken away.
  If no secret vehicles are left, the playlist disappears.
- You can rename the playlist and reorder the vehicles — the mod keeps your title and order
  and only adds new vehicles to the end.
- Carousel playlists are enabled by the server. If they are disabled, the mod does nothing.
